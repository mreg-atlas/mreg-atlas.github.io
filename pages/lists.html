Lists

Connections between signalling and metabolic enzymes, found automatically and compared with the Atlas maps. Each list shows only what the lists before it do not contain. Connections already drawn on an Atlas map are left out.

  1. Metabolic enzymes in Reactome signalling: enzymes of the human metabolic model Human-GEM that take part in Reactome signalling processes, with the proteins that catalyse or regulate each process.
  2. Additions to Atlas maps: enzymes modified by a regulator, from OmniPath, where the enzyme is on an Atlas map and the regulator is not.
  3. Leads for new maps: enzymes modified by a regulator, from OmniPath, where the enzyme is on no Atlas map.

Lists 2 and 3 hold OmniPath connections that Reactome does not contain, each supported by at least two curated databases.
